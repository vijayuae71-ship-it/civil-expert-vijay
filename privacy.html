Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name to answer a question.
  • Your question, and any photo or document you attach, is sent to our AI provider to produce the answer. We don't keep your photos or documents.
  • We keep the text of questions and answers for a limited time to answer repeat questions faster and to improve answers. It is not linked to your name.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your information.
  • You can ask us to delete anything you sent us. See Contact.

1. Who we are

Construction AI Expert ("we") runs this website and, once it is launched, the matching WhatsApp service. The service gives general construction guidance generated by AI, built on the experience of a senior civil engineer.

3. WhatsApp (when launched)

  • Messages to our WhatsApp number pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
  • To understand follow-up questions, we keep your last 8 messages for up to 24 hours, together with your answer-style choice. Your phone number is not stored: it is replaced by a one-way coded key.
  • Photos and documents you send are used for that answer only and are not stored by us.
  • Send new at any time to clear the conversation we keep.

4. Who processes your information for us

  • Anthropic (the Claude AI model) generates the answers from your question and any attachment, under its commercial terms. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts the website and server functions and stores the saved answers, feedback, request counters and Expert review requests.
  • Meta / WhatsApp carries WhatsApp messages, once the WhatsApp service is launched.
  • jsDelivr (a public code library service) supplies the PDF, Excel and PowerPoint export tools. These load only when you tap an export button.

These providers may process data in other countries, such as the United States, to run the service. We don't share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information about you we hold, and correct it;
  • delete your Expert review request, feedback or WhatsApp conversation;
  • stop using your information, and withdraw any consent you gave;
  • raise a complaint. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Because we don't link questions to names, we may ask for the approximate date and wording of a question, or the WhatsApp number you used, to find it.

6. Children

The service is for adults working in construction. It isn't intended for anyone under 18.

7. AI answers

Answers are generated by AI and are general professional guidance only. They can be wrong. Always check design, testing, codes, safety and approvals with the responsible qualified engineer before acting on them. See the full disclaimer & terms of use.

8. Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, use the Expert review form, choose the topic Privacy request, and tell us what you'd like us to do. We aim to reply within 30 days.
