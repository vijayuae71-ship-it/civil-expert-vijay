1. What this service is

Construction AI Expert answers questions about construction, materials, MEP, estimating, QA/QC and site work. It reads photos, drawings and documents you choose to share, and provides calculators and a materials FAQ. The answers are produced by an AI model using general knowledge, built-in reference material and the information you give it. They are not reviewed by a person before you see them.

2. Limits of the answers

  • Not a design or approval. Mix designs, quantities, sizes, spacings and calculations are preliminary starting points. Structural design, load sizing, electrical and fire design, and code compliance must be carried out and signed by the responsible qualified professional.
  • Codes and local rules vary. References to codes (for example IS, BS, EN, NFPA, ACI) are given for general information and may not be the edition, amendment or authority that applies to your project. Your project specification and local authority (for example DEWA, Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence, CPWD or your state authority) take precedence.
  • Rates and costs are indicative. Market rates, preliminary estimates and BOQs change with location, date, supplier and specification. Anything marked “To be verified against applicable CPWD DSR” has not been checked against the schedule. Get current written quotations before pricing, tendering or buying.
  • Photos and drawings. The AI reads only what is visible in the file you share. It can misread unclear text, miss information that isn't shown, or not know about later revisions. A photo is never enough to confirm that a structure is safe.
  • Products and brands. Products, brands or suppliers mentioned in an answer or a photo are examples only, not endorsements. Follow the manufacturer's current data sheet and instructions.
  • Translations. Answers in languages other than English are machine-generated and may lose precision. Where it matters, check the technical terms and numbers.
