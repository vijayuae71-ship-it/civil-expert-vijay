    Comparative statement

    Amounts are recomputed on the tender quantities. Yellow: rate far below the median (check it can be done at that rate). Red: far above.

    Members

    Sizes in mm. Bars: 3-16 = 3 bars of 16 mm, 2-16+1-12 mixed, 10@150 = 10 mm at 150 c/c. Beam: L = clear span, main = bottom, top = top bars. Column: L = height. Slab: L = lx, B = ly, main along lx, top = distribution. Footing: main along L, top = along B.

      Certification

      Site inspection photos

      Take or choose photos; add where each one is. The AI lists every visible defect with its severity, likely cause and remedy. Photos are kept small on this phone for the report.

        Snag list

        Found from photos only: confirm each item on site (crack width and depth, hollowness, strength need measurement or testing). You can edit any cell — tap it.
